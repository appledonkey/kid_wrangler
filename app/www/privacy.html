Kid Wrangler — Privacy

Effective date: TBD on launch

KidWrangler is a parent utility that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. KidWrangler does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

What we don't do

  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or usage data.
  • We do not collect crash reports.
  • We do not contact any server. The app works fully offline.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not allow third parties to collect data from this app.

Children's privacy

KidWrangler is designed for parents, grandparents, and caregivers to use as a tool while supervising children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, the app is compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, US) and GDPR-K (EU).

Data stored on your device

The app remembers your last selected game and your audio preferences locally on your device. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app erases it.

In-app purchases

If you purchase additional games, the transaction is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Apple or Google that a purchase was made, so we can unlock the additional games on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be highlighted at the top.
